# Changelog

## 0.3.2

- The trip plan is stored in the shared history file, so the desktop app shows the same flights.

## 0.3.1

- Listen shelf for music, podcasts and audiobooks.

## 0.3.0

- The planner takes one time per flight for trips with a stop, and remembers the plan.
- Card pictures fill their frame; magazine covers keep their top.
- Books saved as web pages open in the browser.

## 0.2.0

- Big-screen view: artwork for the selected item, large titles and a shelf of picture cards.
- `--window` opens Cabin in its own fullscreen foot window with large type.
- Left/Right browse the shelf; Up/Down change section.
- Colours follow the current Omarchy theme.
- Cabin's window returns to fullscreen and focus after a film, magazine or game closes (Hyprland).
- Documented catalogue format with an example, `--version`, and tests for the interface.

## 0.1.0

- First version: films, magazines, puzzles and Atari ST games from one list.
- Resume progress, a flight planner, a battery readout and `--check` for before a flight.
